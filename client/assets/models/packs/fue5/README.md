# Models from FUE5

These models come from **FUE5** (https://github.com/FUE5BASE/FUE5), a fan remake of Factorio in
Unreal Engine 5 by **Hurricane and Nuke**, whose models and textures are licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0** (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/). They are not under Zenith's GPL-3.0 licence:
these files, and anything made from them, may be shared only under the same licence, with this
credit, and not for commercial use.

Changes made to the originals (`tools/models/fue5.py` and `fue5_blender.py`, from FUE5's
`Content/MyStuff/ENTITIES/STRUCTURES` and `VEHICLES` FBX files and their .jpg textures):
scaled from FUE5's units (2 to a tile) into tiles and evenly to fit the entity's footprint, turned
to face north, centred on the footprint, cut at the ground (or lifted onto it), ground decals
removed, decimated to the client's triangle budget, and every texture baked into one atlas
(embedded in the .glb as JPEG) on a new UV map. `tools/models/bake_fue5_pbr.py` then derives
packed ORM (ambient cavity occlusion, roughness, metallic) and OpenGL tangent-space normal maps
from the clean embedded diffuse textures (adding subtle procedural micro-grain on watermark-replaced
flat surfaces), and `pack.json` sets `"shading": "pbr"` (reversible via `--fue5-pbr=off` or
`--pack-shading=factorio`); everything remains under FUE5's CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 licence.

Sizes: each model is then matched to the size of our model it replaces (`fue5.py` `our_size`, an
axis more than 8% off, all axes alike when that is enough, never narrower than 0.9 of its footprint);
the locomotive and wagons exactly, their wheel sets moved to our bogies (1.5 and 2.5 tiles either
side). The silo (round) and the rocket keep their own. The electric poles and the substation instead
have their insulators moved onto Factorio's wire connection points (client `wires.gd`), copies of
the copper insulator making the big pole's and substation's red and green ones.

This folder is the FUE5 model pack (its `pack.json`). In the packs' default order it is above
Wube3DP and Placeholders (`client/scripts/world/model_packs.gd`), so a model here replaces theirs of
the same name: Placeholders', and Wube3DP's thirty-eight (the electric mining drill, the small electric pole,
the stone and steel furnaces, the wooden, iron and steel chests, the five logistic chests, the 21 stone wall pieces, the
belt and its two curves, the underground belt's entrance and exit, whose faster tiers Wube3DP draws as the yellow one repainted), the four splitters
(Placeholders' one, its faster tiers repainted), and its six
inserters (Wube3DP's four direction files each; FUE5's one is turned), which stay
in their pack. The Model
packs window changes the order or takes any one model from another pack. Ours stay in their
packs (`../placeholders/`, `../wube3dp/`) and in their Blender recipes. Delete a file here to go
back to ours.

The pack's picture in the Model packs window (`pack.json` `icon_image`, `logo.png`) is the FUE5
project's logo, used at the owner's choice and credited to the FUE5 project
(https://github.com/FUE5BASE/FUE5, Hurricane and Nuke). Zenith reads it as one of the project's
assets, under its CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 asset licence (its LICENCE.md), like the models here.
