# Zenith 0.2.0

Everything since 0.1.3 (0.1.4 was never released publicly, so its changes are here too).

## Highlights

- **Zenith runs Factorio's belts itself:** items on belts move exactly as the game moves them, and
  full belts are drawn full.
- **New 3D models by David (Vasillo):** textured belts, undergrounds, splitters, inserters and the
  burner drill, in the download and used by default.
- **The Cinematic look comes with Zenith:** filmic colour, soft shadows, drifting clouds, sunrises
  and sunsets. Switch it off in the Add-ons menu for the Factorio look.
- **Shading you can see:** buildings sit on the ground with Factorio's dark rim round their feet,
  and corners and gaps are shaded, in daylight too.
- **Screenshots up to 8K** with F12, of the exact moment you pressed it, saved to Pictures\Zenith.

## Upgrading

This version needs its own bridge mod (in the download). With an older bridge the belts near you
stay empty, and Zenith says so.

## Belts

- Full lanes are drawn as solid rows of their item, piled as high as the stack, as in Factorio.
- Belts stop only when the circuit or logistic network switches them off, as in Factorio.
- Fast belts no longer strobe or seem to run backwards, and each tier's arrows are spaced as
  Factorio spaces them.
- Stacked items lie in messy piles, near and far alike.
- Splitters and undergrounds get the belt's start and end pieces where a line begins or ends.

## Graphics

- **Ambient occlusion:** fades as you zoom out and switches off far out, so it costs nothing there.
  Contact shading has its own setting, on in every preset.
- **Belts cast their shadows again**, and with Terrain Elevation's hills on, low buildings (belts, pipes,
  inserters, assemblers) cast theirs again too: their shadows had been drawn under the raised ground.
  Pipes and walls get a light contact band at their foot, and ambient occlusion is stronger in sunlight and
  shows further out.
- **3D grass** near the character, from Factorio's own pictures, and none on concrete, paths or
  under buildings (Settings > Graphics > 3D grass).
- **View distance up to 50 chunks** (far views cost a lot; the setting turns yellow, then red).
- **Space** is near black as in Factorio, and the planet below a platform turns once per its day.
- **Fulgora's lightning** is drawn, with its flash and sound.
- **Weapons light the ground:** flamethrower flames and muzzle flashes.
- **Full screen at any resolution:** the world drawn at the Resolution you pick, the interface sharp.
- **Less flicker far away:** models cross-fade to their simpler versions.
- **Rockets launch as in Factorio:** the lights, doors, flames, smoke and sounds, and the camera
  rides up with the rocket.
- Models redone from Factorio's pictures: the electromagnetic plant, the electric furnace and
  pentapods' shells.

## Screenshots

With the Cinematic camera add-on (in the download), F12:

- takes shots up to 8K, holding the moment while it's taken (a progress bar shows; F12 or Esc
  cancels);
- waits for the far ground to arrive when you're zoomed far out;
- saves to Pictures\Zenith, with an Open folder button.

## Interface

- **Train schedules** in Factorio's layout: stops in frames, drag to reorder, AND / OR conditions,
  interrupts. The hub's schedule too.
- **F1's help** lists every control under the key it's bound to now, with each add-on's keys.
- **Dragging** undergrounds and pipes-to-ground places pairs at full reach; poles at wire reach.
- **Crafting:** a craft shows in the queue at once, its pie moves smoothly, and tooltips show Total raw.
- Train stops wear their colour; plain pipes show their fluid every few tiles in Alt-mode.

## Settings

- **SMAA is the new default anti-aliasing:** sharper than FXAA, and a little cheaper.
- **No MSAA with the Cinematic look:** its 2x, 4x and 8x choices are greyed out while the look is on
  (they fringed every edge with it), and a saved MSAA is switched to SMAA. Ambient occlusion and Clouds
  are greyed out under it too, since its own settings (Add-ons menu) decide them.
- **Model look is gone:** models always wear Factorio's colours (the projected-sprite look is removed).
- **Every setting checked in the game:** Alt-mode in Settings now switches Alt-mode in the game, the
  minimap's size and zoom sliders are saved, and Resolution's Default puts the window back to 1600 × 900.

## Performance

- **The new belt models far away** are drawn simpler, with simpler shadows. On a copy of a big base
  drawn with them: standing, the graphics card's time a frame went from about 14.6 ms to 13-14 ms;
  running laps, the middle frame went from 12.5 to 10.8 ms and frames over 33 ms fell by about a
  quarter.
- Fewer long frames in the first minute after joining a big base.
- The Cinematic look asks more of the graphics card than the Factorio look; on a weaker card,
  switch it off. View distances past 20 chunks, and forests, cost the most.

## Fixes

- The bridge keeps the game running if one of its jobs fails (`/f3d-status` lists it), and saves from
  older versions start cleanly.
- Things a script adds or removes quietly no longer stay drawn or go missing.
- Hills (Terrain Elevation add-on) no longer vanish at the screen's edges, and cost about what they
  did in 0.1.3 standing still.
- Hiding the interface (F11) hides alert and no-power icons too.
- No more white streak in the sky, dark specks with MSAA, or black boxes on Aquilo's and Gleba's water.
- Rockets no longer rise out of bare ground or stand at the spawn; cargo pods land on their spot.
- Two waits that could freeze Zenith (Ctrl+F9's switch, a new hills field) are gone.
- Zenith no longer crashes changing surface to a planet with a belt loop that side-loads onto itself
  (seen on Aquilo).

## Known issues

- **Big bases may run below 60 UPS:** the bridge's work for the new belt simulation is heavier (about
  53 UPS on our big test base, where earlier versions held 59-60). A fix is planned for a hotfix.
- **Belts:** about 96% of the items in view on a big base are drawn, and a few are drawn where the
  game has none. Close up, belt items drawn as models can merge into strips seen from above.
- **Screenshots zoomed far out** have just been reworked to wait for every chunk, and haven't been
  tested in the game yet.
- **The new shading in daylight** has only been checked in a test world, not yet on a real save.
- **Restart Zenith after changing anti-aliasing:** changing it while playing can leave it much slower.
- **Running on a big base** still has more slow frames than standing; forests are heavy on High.
- Solar panels look grey at sunset in the Cinematic look.
- Mods' own windows are still off by default: use them in Factorio's window (Ctrl+F9).
- Hovering a chest doesn't list its contents, and Tips and tricks have no pictures.

## For modellers and add-on makers

- Rigged inserter models are driven by Zenith's arm, pincers and all.
- Textured models far away are simplified with their textures kept, and their moving parts and baked
  occlusion are kept too.
- Add-on API 1.6: screenshots, full detail and holding chunks for long flights; looks can have their
  own sunrise and sunset (docs/ADDONS.md).
