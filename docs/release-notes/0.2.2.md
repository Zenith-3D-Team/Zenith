# Zenith 0.2.2

Everything since 0.2.1: the fixes from a recorded play session on 0.2.1 and two play-test lists
after it, model packs, and many new models. How things work is in the user guide, `docs/USER-GUIDE.md`.

## Quick start

Download the right version for your platform, extract it, and run Zenith. When upgrading from an earlier version,
Zenith launches with the matching bridge mod (0.2.2) and updates automatically in game.

**Your models folder:** the textured models in the download are now one zip, `models/Zenith.zip` (the Zenith pack).
An older `Default.zip` there still works and is read as the Zenith pack; loose .glb files in the folder are one
more pack, "Loose models".

**An extra Windows download, Zenith-0.2.2-dev-windows.zip,** is the same build plus the debug menu (Ctrl+F12 in a
game) and the main menu's performance test. The debug menu's time, speed, pause and teleport controls follow
Factorio's console rule: in a multiplayer game only admins can use them.

## Model packs

- **Zenith pack (bundled in `models/Zenith.zip`, 108 models):** Vasillo's textured models for all belt tiers (with
  updated PBR belt surface textures), splitters, undergrounds, inserters, burner and electric mining drills, pipes,
  scattered ore rocks, and item models.
- **Model packs:** every zip or folder in `models` is one; order and preview them in their window.
- **Add-ons and Model packs** now have their own windows, out of Settings.
- **A building's Model button** and its hover panel show and change its pack.
- **Factorio pack:** buildings drawn as Factorio's pictures on cards.
- **FUE5 (Adapted), 142 models:** now belts, inserters, robots, pipes, walls and signals.
- **Packs' own shading:** a pack can ask for its textured models to be drawn as their files say (`"shading": "pbr"`
  in its pack.json; Vasillo's do), lit as Blender and Godot light them instead of with the balance made for
  Factorio's pictures. Settings > Graphics > Advanced > Packs' own shading, Factorio's everywhere, draws them as before.

## New

- **Vehicles:** headlights, Ctrl+click to fill, their guns, repair packs mend them.
- **One stack in the hand,** as in Factorio; counts drop the moment you build.
- **Building:** drags stop at what you hold; undergrounds remove the run between; walls turn corners.
- **Oil, ore and pipes:** placement hints, fluid icons, pipeline marks, too-long warning, ore amounts.
- **Research queue:** starting a technology queues its missing prerequisites (shown orange).
- **Mods:** ticked mods load with a save; **Sync mods with save** keeps exactly its own.
- **Join your Zenith world from Factorio** and take over your character.
- **Steadier, sharper shadows;** lamps light about twice as far.
- **Menu backdrop:** a new view behind the main menu; set your own from any game (Esc).
- **Appearance and lighting:** matte ground, grass and trees; night vision sounds, icon and screen-edge glow;
  Cinematic shaders 0.5.3 with noon north-west sunlight, tuned ambient occlusion, and pipe covers on open fluid ports.
- **Performance:** binary bridge messages and GPU shader optimizations across items, shadows, sky and vegetation.

## Fixes

- **Belts:** F stops when let go; packed belts close up and start as in the game; dropping an item with Z shows at once.
- **Mining:** no swing out of reach; no pickaxe sound on buildings; drill uranium refusal shown as in Factorio.
- **Alt-mode:** turret ammo, cargo wagon contents, and clearer icons drawn in front by distance.
- **Ctrl+click with an empty hand** takes what Factorio's does, no longer fuel.
- **Models:** closed belt ends, a glowing burner inserter, wheels roll right.
- **Also:** F6/F7 views, screenshots, Esc past tooltips, lab box, alert sounds, splitters, zero-light blackout fix.

## Known issues

- **Factorio pack cards** have no shadows; **headlights** light only your own vehicle.
- **Slow moments:** a big zip's first unpacking; two seconds after a far-out screenshot.
- **Building:** a red ghost at reach's edge can build; undo skips belts undergrounds removed.
- **Still open from 0.2.1:** forests cost the graphics card most; restart after changing anti-aliasing.
