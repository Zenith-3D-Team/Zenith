# Zenith 0.3.0

Everything since 0.2.2: weather, a long view to the horizon, new cliffs, Immersive Enemies, combat that looks and
sounds like a fight, a big round of performance work (much of the client in native code, megabases, smoother frames
while moving), new lighting and Vasillo's PBR models. How things work is in the user guide, `docs/USER-GUIDE.md`.

## Quick start

Download the right version for your platform, extract it, and run Zenith. Zenith launches with the matching bridge mod
(0.3.0) and updates it automatically in game. **Zenith-0.3.0-dev-windows.zip** is the same build plus the debug menu
(Ctrl+F12) and the performance test, for testers.

## New

- **Immersive Weather** (bundled add-on, on): skies, wind that roars in gusts, 3D rain that fills the view when zoomed
  out, wet ground, rocks, biters and tanks, puddles, thunderstorms with lightning and thunder. Settings → Add-ons.
- **Immersive Enemies** (bundled add-on): biter, spitter, worm and nest ragdolls, wounds, acid spit and burning corpses,
  simulated in native code; a horde's deaths are spread over a few frames.
- **Combat:** laser beams and bullet tracers, flamethrower streams from the nozzle, muzzle flashes and explosions that
  light the area and cast shadows, the missing combat sounds; biters notice you in a car, tank or spidertron.
- **Vehicles:** rebuilt car and tank models with suspension, steering and recoil, puddle splashes and tread tracks,
  headlights that come on in storms; a brake-and-hold option (Settings → Interface).
- **Distant horizon:** the far view draws the rest of your base out to the horizon, with far lamps and signals.
- **Render distance and level of detail** in one slider each, in a redone Graphics settings page; **AMD FSR 2.2**.
- **Lighting:** sky light, a fill light, ambient occlusion (Cinematic), matte ground, grass and trees, brighter night
  vision, and real darkness at sun 0 and ambient 0.
- **Cliffs as one wall** seated in the terrain; **planets' clouds from space**.
- **Vasillo's Zenith pack:** his models with their own PBR materials, rocks and ores, walls, gates and ammo; PBR or
  Factorio's shading per model (Settings → Model packs). Biters' materials fixed (no flat or shiny look).
- **Trains and signals on elevated rails**; **characters' corpses** drawn, mineable, with a skull on the map.
- **New maps without Space Age** (issue #28); when map creation fails, Factorio's own error is shown.
- **Main menu:** a new default backdrop (a space platform over Aquilo), backdrops taken in space show space and the
  planet below, and a **Check for updates** button (asks only when clicked; auto check off by default).
- **A loading screen** while shaders are prepared; a new version clears old shader caches on its first start.
- Cargo wagons show their items in alt-mode; items dropped on a belt with Z show at once; FUE5's pumpjack moves.
- **Terrain Elevation 0.1.11** (bundled add-on, now on by default, with Immersive Weather and Immersive Enemies): hills
  kept on disk, faster maths.
- **One download for everyone:** `--dev-tools` turns on the testing tools (performance test, debug menu).

## Performance

- Native code for the belt simulation, inserter arms, movers, decoratives, chunk building, far-view meshing, belt
  items, message decoding and Terrain Elevation's maths.
- Fewer frame spikes while moving: work spread over frames, off-screen things unloaded sooner, rendering on its own
  thread, alt-mode icons on the graphics card, chunk loading without pop-in; the spike log names the slowest add-ons.
- Faster loading of large model packs, with less memory.
- Megabases: packed (binary) messages, priority queues, whole-base jobs spread across ticks, a paced garbage collector.

## Changes and fixes

- Zenith follows Factorio 2.1's own default keys; Settings → Controls → **Zenith 0.2.2's keys** brings the old ones back.
- Joining on a slow PC: no restart loop, the connect wait counts only running time, and the shader-cache restart no
  longer leaves a second window frozen.
- Gates open when Factorio opens them; health bars hide with the interface; the surfaces panel scrolls.
- Storage tanks no longer open a level window by default (`--tank-window=on`); the main menu is held at 60 FPS.
- The character no longer bends over cliffs; no pits under vehicles on hills.
- A dedicated server that never answers: the message now names `auto_pause`.
- The bridge mod needs Factorio 2.1.

## Known issues

- Mods' own windows are still off by default.
- Very large forests and the longest view distances are still the heaviest load on the GPU.
- A save from 0.2.2 can show a tall stick on inserters on its first load; rotating any one inserter clears it for good.
- The first start of a new version prepares its shaders for a minute or more; later starts are faster.
- Memory use is higher than 0.2.2 (7-9 GB in a busy base); 16 GB of RAM is recommended.
