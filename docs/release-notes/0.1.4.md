# Zenith 0.1.4

*Pre-release.* The full performance figures come with the release.

## Rockets and space

- **Factorio's rocket launch:** a silo's launch now plays out as it does in Factorio. The red lights
  blink, the doors open wide, the rocket rises up the shaft lit from inside by its engines, with
  Factorio's own jet, glare, smoke columns and sounds from your install, sits on the pad for 8
  seconds and then climbs at the game's pace. Riding a rocket up to a platform starts from the shaft
  too, and the camera now goes up with the rocket (it had stayed on the ground) and eases back at the
  end.
- **Planets glide in a platform's sky:** the planet below a travelling platform moves smoothly instead
  of stepping once a second. It comes down the view from ahead as you arrive, sinks away behind as you
  leave, shrinks as it gets further off, and fades out at the far end of the route.
- **Rockets in the right place:** rockets no longer rise out of bare ground near the spawn (launches
  on other planets were drawn on the planet you were looking at), no rocket stands at the spawn after
  loading a save, a launch no longer shows an extra cargo pod, and pods come straight down onto their
  landing spot.

## Trains and building

- **Train schedules in Factorio's shape:** train and platform schedules are one list, as in Factorio:
  each stop in its own frame with its go button, name and delete button, conditions in Factorio's
  words ("30 s passed", "Cargo [item] < 100") with AND / OR between them, stops dragged to reorder,
  and interrupts folded to a row that opens. The hub's schedule works the same way.
- **Train stops wear their colour** on the top of the model, where Factorio paints it (red when nobody
  painted them, as in Factorio), and a repaint shows at once.
- **Dragging undergrounds and poles:** dragging underground belts or pipes-to-ground places them in
  pairs at their full reach, facing along the line. Electric poles of every size are placed at their
  wire reach: the big pole and the substation are measured from where they really stand (the line
  could start a tile off and leave the next pole out of reach), and a pole already on the line carries
  the line on.

## Crafting and windows

- **The crafting queue:** a craft shows in the queue the moment you start it when you have its
  ingredients, and the game's own queue takes over a moment later. The progress pie moves every frame
  and sweeps evenly round the slot instead of jumping.
- **Total raw:** a recipe's tooltip lists Factorio's Total raw, broken down through the hand recipes
  ("Too complex to calculate" for loops, as in Factorio).
- **Train overview:** shows a loading icon until its list arrives, and far trains' views show one
  until their map does.
- **Pipes in Alt-mode:** plain pipes show their fluid icon only every few tiles, on a fixed pattern,
  instead of on every pipe. Pipes-to-ground, pumps and tanks still show theirs every time.

## The look

- **Sunrises and sunsets:** in the Cinematic look the sun now sets in the west, its light reddening and
  following it down with long shadows, through a blue hour into moonlight, each planet with its own
  dusk and dawn. In the Factorio look the ground and light stay as before and the sky shows a
  restrained sunset. In both, lamps come on gradually as it gets dark.
- **The Cinematic look comes with Zenith:** the Cinematic shaders add-on is now in the download and
  switched on: filmic colour, bloom on lights and fire, soft shadows, depth haze, clouds drifting in
  the sky with their shadows on the ground, a real sunrise and sunset with dawn mist and a blue hour,
  and temporal anti-aliasing so far detail holds still. Its settings (clouds, cloud cover, Sunrise and
  sunset and more) are in the Add-ons menu; switch the add-on off there for the Factorio look.
- **3D grass:** grass and low plants near the character stand up in 3D, each from its own Factorio
  picture (Settings > Graphics > 3D grass; off on the Low preset, 30 tiles on Medium, 40 with wind on
  High, 50 on Ultra). Factorio's own view still shows them as Factorio draws them.
- **Full screen at any resolution:** in full screen and borderless, the Resolution setting now draws
  the world at that size scaled to fill the monitor, while the interface stays sharp and laid out as
  at the chosen size. Windowed is as before.
- **Fulgora's lightning** is drawn: a forked bolt from the sky to each strike, a flash of light, its
  sound and the sky lighting up.
- **No more dark specks with MSAA:** with MSAA anti-aliasing on, buildings had dark specks and flicker,
  worst far away. They're gone.
- **Lights from weapons:** flamethrower flames and muzzle flashes light the ground.

## Models

- **The electromagnetic plant** rebuilt from Factorio's picture at its true proportions: two thin blue
  arches over an open middle with its turntable, cogs and spinning wire ball, the cream towers, the
  hose ring and the brass elbow, in the sprite's darker colours.
- **The electric furnace** brought closer to Factorio's: its chimney and fans down to the sprite's
  height, the grille box it lacked, the heater lit only while it works (it was always lit), and its
  colours graded to the sprite's steel and teal.
- **Pentapods' shells** (Gleba's stompers) fitted to Factorio's shell picture: its outline, knobs, pits,
  crack and spikes, coloured from it.
- **Turrets** drawn with the textured models turn to aim again.
- **Rails:** the light ballast stones sit clear of the bed and nearer its colour, so they no longer
  flicker against it.

## Belts

- **Belts always run, unless switched off:** a belt or loader stands still only when the circuit or
  logistic network switches it off, and starts again at once when it's switched back on, as in
  Factorio. A splitter's gears turn while items pass through it.
- **Fast belts no longer strobe:** the slats and chevrons of red, blue and green belts are smeared over
  their travel between frames, so at 60 fps they read as moving instead of flickering or seeming to
  run backwards (turbo belts' chevron outlines still flicker a little). Yellow belts and belts that are
  switched off look as before.
- **Messy piles everywhere:** stacked items on belts are piled with each copy turned a little, the way
  models were, now for icons too and for the far belts' icons (they were square columns).
- Items on belts move as they did in 0.1.3. Zenith running Factorio's own belt rules itself is coming
  in 0.1.5.

## For modellers

- **Belt line ends:** a `transport-belt-start` and `transport-belt-end` model in the models folder are
  drawn where a line of belts begins and ends.
- **Textured belts scroll again** (they stood still), and their curves no longer creep sideways into
  a fan of fine lines at the inner corners. A normal texture that is really the belt's colour picture
  is left out (curves had come out pale or glaring).

## For add-on makers

- **Add-on API 1.5:** a look can have its own sunrise and sunset (`twilight`, `sky_twilight`,
  `at_twilight`, `at_dawn`, `fog_from_sky`), its passes get the sun's height, direction and colours,
  and `api.shaders.sun()` says where the sun is. Since 1.4 a look also owns the clouds, temporal
  anti-aliasing and the roughness limiter it changes, so changing a Graphics setting no longer undoes
  it, and switching the add-on off brings back what it replaced (docs/ADDONS.md).
- **Space:** a look's passes are told when the view is on a space platform, and the Cinematic look
  keeps its clouds off there.

## Fixes and changes

- **The bridge stays up:** if one of the bridge's jobs fails, it is logged and listed in
  `/f3d-status`, and the game and the rest of the bridge go on.
- **Saves from older versions:** a save made with an older bridge starts clean when you load it with
  this one (its old connections and half-done jobs are dropped), instead of stopping the game.
  Checked with saves from 0.1.1 and 0.1.2.
- **Things a script removes or makes:** trees and buildings that a mod or a console command removes
  or creates without telling other mods no longer stay drawn (or missing): Zenith notices the change
  within about a second.
- **The sky at some times of day:** a white streak up the sky and black ground at certain times of
  day are gone.
- **Hills (Terrain Elevation add-on):** the ground at the edges of the screen no longer disappears too
  early, and what is just out of view is culled by the hills' real height, so hills cost about what
  they did in 0.1.3 standing still.
- **Hiding the interface** (F11 with the Cinematic camera): alert and no-power icons over buildings
  hide with it.

## Known issues

- **Solar panels look grey at sunset in the Cinematic look:** it's the low, warm sun on their blue
  texture, not a reflection; not fixed yet.
- **Running at speed on a big base** still has more slow frames than standing still; forests also
  cost the graphics card a lot on High. Both are being worked on.
- **Items on belts close up** drawn as 3D models can merge into solid strips seen from above.
- **Mods' own windows** are still off by default (Settings > Interface > Show mods' windows): use them
  in Factorio's window (the Factorio button at the top left, or Ctrl+F9).
- **Hovering a chest** doesn't list its contents yet, and the Tips and tricks pages have no pictures.
- **Walking on hills (Terrain Elevation add-on)** still draws about twice the triangles 0.1.3 did, at
  about the same number of draw calls, so frames there take longer than in 0.1.3. Without hills (the
  add-on is off unless you switch it on) it doesn't apply.

## Performance

The full before-and-after figures against 0.1.3 come with the release. What a test on a copy of a
big base showed before the last fixes, without hills: frames about as quick as 0.1.3's (a little
quicker standing, even with 3D grass), the graphics card drawing fewer triangles, and the game holding
60 UPS. Not all better: Zenith's bridge does about 5-6% more work per tick, and more of its ticks run
a little over 16.7 ms, from its two new watches (belts switched off by the circuit network, and things
changed without an event). The Cinematic look, on by default, does more work on the graphics card
than the Factorio look: on a PC short of graphics power, switch it off in the Add-ons menu.

What changed:

- **Shadows of belt items close up only:** items on belts cast shadows only with the camera within
  16 tiles of them.
- **Lighter model shading:** the weathering noise is skipped where it can't show and fades out where
  it is finer than a pixel.
- **Belts at 4K:** far belts switch to their simpler versions at the same distance as at 1080p.
- **Hills culled by their height:** see Fixes and changes.
- **Mods' windows** are only read by the bridge for a client that draws them.
