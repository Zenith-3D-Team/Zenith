# Zenith 0.3.1

Everything since 0.3.0: Direct3D 12 on Windows, four waves of performance work aimed at big bases, the logistics network as in Factorio 2.1, a rebuilt rocket launch, animated
assembling machines, Gleba's enemies and about sixty play-test fixes. How things work is in the user guide,
`docs/USER-GUIDE.md`.

## Quick start

Download the right version for your platform, extract it, and run Zenith. Zenith launches with the matching bridge mod
(0.3.1) and updates it automatically in game. All five add-ons are bundled and on; `--dev-tools` turns on the testing
tools (performance test, debug menu).

## Performance

About as fast as 0.3.0 on the same test base: level on a fast desktop; on a slower PC level on the main-bus run with
fewer slow bridge frames, and slower only standing still among many inserters (see Known issues).

- **Big bases:** far view, world streaming, alt-mode, inserter arms and belt motion moved off the main thread or into
  native code; most of the per-frame work on a megabase is gone or spread out.
- **Distant horizon:** retiring far meshes, visibility and the background scan no longer stall a frame after you move.
- **World streaming:** chunks are added in slices, pipe covers worked out once, the disk cache saved in small steps.
- **Alt-mode:** a new height field moves the icons instead of rebuilding them; unchanged chunks cost nothing; missing
  icons load in the background.
- **Inserters, belts and trains:** arms re-bind only when their model really changes, parked trains are skipped, belt
  motion vectors are uploaded only where they changed.
- **Shaders** compile in the background at low priority, with a loading screen that shows the time left.

## New

- **Upscaling** lists AMD FSR 3.1 and 4.1, NVIDIA DLSS and Automatic with an **Upscaling quality** row; in this
  download they are greyed out ("Not in this build": they need Zenith's own engine build, coming in a later release).
  AMD FSR 2.2 stays the default and works as before.
- **Direct3D 12** is the Windows default, with about half the memory of Vulkan; Vulkan stays under Settings → Graphics
  → Renderer.
- **Logistics network as in Factorio 2.1:** request sections with multipliers, status tints, the networks window (L),
  network details on hover, the missing logistics alerts.
- **Rocket launch** rebuilt: blast doors, the shaft, hold-down clamps, the plume and smoke (`--rocket-vfx=old`).
- **Assembling machines 1-3** rebuilt with animations.
- **Personal roboport robots** fly back into your armour; flying robots show what they carry in alt-mode.
- **Immersive Enemies 0.1.3:** Gleba's wrigglers, strafers, stompers and spawners.
- **Immersive Weather 0.3.5:** softer rain and wind; a separate **Wind** volume slider. **Cinematic shaders 0.6.3:**
  faster clouds.
- **Terrain Elevation 0.1.12:** no more freezes from its log lines.
- **Map:** a top-bar 2D/3D toggle and a scroll-to-3D bar past the farthest zoom.
- **Inserters hold stacks** in their hands; an **armour power bar** beside the armour slot.
- **Ctrl+C** copies into your inventory, library and books; a tabbed item picker for the quickbar; "Made in:" icons.

## Changes and fixes

- About sixty play-test fixes: drag placement, ghosts and previews, pipe drag and pipe caps, rail signals and train
  stops, turret alignment, belt and inserter timing, item drops with Z, chest limits, shift-drag transfers.
- Inserter arms move within 24 tiles of you, as in 0.3.0, and rest with the same model and shadows past that;
  `--live-inserter-radius=view` moves every arm you can see (up to 64 tiles, slower).
- Inserters from older saves no longer show a tall stick on their first load.
- A model read in the background can no longer hang the game (on quit or after a pack change).
- Model choices by default: Placeholders' biters and spawners, FUE5's lab and pumpjack (for new players and anyone
  who never picked a model; your own choices stay; `--default-model-choices=off`).
- `/ignore` prints Factorio's own lines; Next weapon skips guns without ammo; capsule cooldowns hold across clicks.
- The wind keeps the Ambient volume you set in 0.3.0 (its own Wind slider is new).
- Rail and chain signals no longer light their surroundings (`--signal-omni-lights=on`); train-stop names show in the
  map view.
- The add-on API is at level 1.95.
- The shader loading screen has a **Skip** button: it first warns that the remaining shaders then compile while you
  play (short stutters), with **Wait** (and the time left) or **Skip** to start at once; the next start warms the
  rest (`--boot-skip=off` hides it).

## Known issues

- Mods' own windows are still off by default.
- Very large forests and the longest view distances are still the heaviest load on the GPU.
- The first start of a new version prepares its shaders for a minute or more (several minutes on slower PCs); later
  starts are faster.
- Standing still among many inserters can be up to 5 ms a frame slower than 0.3.0 on slower PCs: resting arms are now
  full 3D models with shadows. `--rest-arm-shadows=tier` and `--rest-arm-basic=stick` trade some of that look for speed.
