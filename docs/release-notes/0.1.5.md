# Zenith 0.1.5 (draft)

Draft notes from `feature/belt-sim-int`, kept when main's 0.1.4 shipped without the belt
simulation. The figures below are from earlier rounds (2026-11-10) and need measuring again
with the live-draw check (tools/belt_view_check.py, section L) before release.

## Belts

- **Zenith runs Factorio's belts itself:** the client keeps its own copy of the game's belt rules and
  moves every item on the belts it is sent, so items move as the game moves them instead of gliding
  between sampled places, and the bridge only sends what the belts can't know (what inserters, drills,
  splitters and circuits change, and where lines leading off the moving area stand). On Windows it runs
  in C++. On a copy of a big base, standing at its main belts, items move out to the whole belt item
  distance (160 tiles) at 59 UPS, where the build before moved them out to 14 tiles; at 24 tiles the
  bridge spends 1.5 ms a tick on belt items against that build's 2.3. Not yet as accurate as the
  build before with Belt updates on Unlimited: standing at those belts, 7.6% of the game's items had
  none drawn near against 1.4% (23% before a fix to how the bridge's reads are applied; on Automatic
  both draw what they reach correctly). Two more fixes since (full lines no longer drain past splitters
  and out of underground belts) halve that in replays of the same base, and on Automatic it now misses
  fewer items than the build before (4.1% against 7.9%); with Unlimited still 7.8% against 1.1%, and
  while running items on belts just coming into view show late (a quarter missing against 2%).
- **Items on belts drawn by the graphics card:** each item on a belt is a small record the item
  shaders move along its lane, so an update writes only the items that changed and the client keeps
  up with many more moving items (on a copy of a big base, about 24,000 moving items: each update's
  work 287 -> 128 ms; standing at the main belts, 9.9 -> 7.4 ms).
- **Items in undergrounds:** items passing under a crossing belt are no longer drawn standing on
  that belt at the tunnel's start.
- **Straight runs moved by the client** are in this build but off by default (`--belt-runs` turns
  them on): standing still, they still draw more items where the game has none than without.
